A few little things to know

What can I download?

Pocket tries public Reels, feed videos and legacy IGTV links. Photos and complete carousels depend on the media Instagram exposes and may be incomplete. Stories and Highlights are best-effort: Instagram often requires a login, and Pocket never asks for credentials or bypasses private access. Profile pictures and private posts aren’t supported.

How do I save a video on my phone or computer?

Copy a direct link to a public Instagram video or Reel you own or have permission to save, paste it into Pocket, then choose an available quality and download. If only Original is shown, no other quality was returned. Your browser controls where the file is saved; check its downloads list or your device’s Downloads folder. A file may not appear automatically in Photos or Gallery. You do not need an Instagram password, login cookie or browser extension.

Is it really free?

Yes. There’s no paid tier, sign-up or daily download quota. Short-term traffic and file-size safeguards keep this server usable for everyone. There are no forced ads or paywalls.

Why won’t a link work sometimes?

Instagram can restrict automated public access, even for public posts. A post may also be private, deleted or expired. If a resolved link expires, submit the Instagram link again. Temporary rate, resource or host limits can also prevent a download. Try again later or try another public link; Pocket cannot guarantee every link will resolve.

What quality and audio formats are available?

Pocket shows the files and quality options returned by Instagram. If only Original is returned, it does not invent other resolutions or upscale video. There is no added watermark, but marks already present in the source remain. Source audio may be absent; MP3 is available only when the deployed server and the media provide a usable audio track. Only save or reuse media you’re authorized to use. See our terms of use for permissions and limitations.
